# Apon Mon Presentation Polish Design

## Goal

Make the SIH deck sound credible and human, while making its system story immediately understandable to judges.

## Direction

Retain the current dark teal-and-gold visual language and 10-slide structure. Replace dense, tool-centric copy with short statements about people, value, and evidence. Keep technical terms only where they substantiate feasibility.

## Key Change

Slide 4 becomes a left-to-right operational flow: person and ASHA worker use the offline app; session data and memories stay on device; optional on-device/edge assistance enriches interaction; a secure outbox synchronizes only when connectivity returns; the PHC receives a concise report. This makes the offline-first promise visible rather than implied.

## Constraints

- Preserve the existing 16:9 printable HTML deck and 10-slide count.
- Use no new dependency or external asset.
- Describe the product as a cognitive-engagement and care-support tool, never a diagnostic device.
- Do not modify unrelated working-tree changes.
