# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Primary users are older adults living with mild, moderate, or severe dementia in North East India. They may have reduced attention, memory, dexterity, vision, literacy, or confidence with technology. Family caregivers and ASHA workers use the same device to prepare familiar content, review changes, support daily routines, and share clear reports with qualified clinicians.

## Product Purpose

Apon Mon provides culturally familiar cognitive games, daily memory support, and explainable local progress tracking in places where connectivity can be unreliable. Success means an older adult can begin a gentle activity without confusion, a caregiver can personalize it with familiar people and routines, and both can understand that results support care conversations but never diagnose dementia.

## Brand Personality

Calm, familiar, dignified. The product should feel like patient support from a trusted family member in a bright North Eastern home: encouraging without being childish, culturally rooted without becoming decorative, and clear without feeling clinical.

## Anti-references

- Generic memory-game apps built from abstract shapes with no personal meaning.
- Loud arcade interfaces with countdown pressure, punishment, flashing effects, or competitive leaderboards.
- Cold hospital dashboards that treat people as scores or imply diagnosis.
- Childlike interfaces that reduce an older adult's dignity.
- Dense admin screens, ambiguous icon-only actions, deceptive live-service claims, and decorative glassmorphism.

## Design Principles

1. One gentle decision at a time. Every screen makes its next action obvious.
2. Familiarity strengthens participation. Prefer family, routines, places, language, music, and culture over abstraction.
3. Encourage effort, never punish difficulty. Retries remain calm and stars only move upward.
4. Explain every score in plain language. Personal baselines matter more than comparison with others.
5. Local ownership stays visible. Offline status, storage, export, reset, and demo limitations are explicit.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Use large touch targets, keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, text alternatives, translated on-screen instructions, optional read-aloud, high-contrast and large-text modes, and reduced-motion behavior. Severe-stage activities use two choices, very large controls, one prompt per screen, and no timers. Meaning never relies on color, charts, audio, or motion alone.
