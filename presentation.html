The Rural Dementia Challenge: Alienation & Lack of Tools

Over 8.8 million Indians live with dementia, yet 90% in rural regions remain undetected and unsupported.

Barrier 1: Cultural & Linguistic
Western & Abstract Games
  • Existing cognitive apps rely on sterile shapes, neon timers, and English text.
  • Older rural adults experience intimidation, agitation, and immediate abandonment.
  • No integration of regional heritage (Bihu, Majuli pottery, Loktak weaving, Hornbill).
Consequence: High cognitive resistance and non-compliance.
Barrier 2: Connectivity & Devices
Unreliable Internet & Cloud Lock
  • North East hilly & riverine terrains have frequent network dropouts.
  • Cloud-heavy apps fail entirely during power cuts or remote village field visits.
  • High server subscription costs exclude rural primary healthcare centers.
Consequence: Zero accessibility in remote ASHA circles.
Barrier 3: Diagnostic Stigma
Caregiver Burden & Stigma
  • Clinical questionnaires induce fear of "failing a mental test".
  • Caregivers lack structured, dignified ways to stimulate loved ones daily.
  • No explainable local tracking to assist doctors during periodic reviews.
Consequence: Delayed intervention & severe caregiver burnout.

SLIDE 04 / 10

System Architecture: Multi-Tier Edge & Offline Pipeline

Complete decoupling of offline client logic from optional edge AI enhancement microservices.

Architecture Pillars
  • Layer 1 (UI Shell): React 18 + Vite PWA + Service Worker cache for zero-bandwidth asset delivery.
  • Layer 2 (State Engine): platform.js manages localStorage attempts, settings, and dynamic adaptive levels.
  • Layer 3 (Private Media): mediaStore.js stores encrypted Blob photos and voice clips in IndexedDB.
  • Layer 4 (Sync Hub): syncQueue.js maintains an offline outbox queue with auto-reconnection flushing.
  • Layer 5 (AI Service): FastAPI microservice hosting Faster Whisper STT & Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite.

Adaptive Engine & Memory Gap Map Analytics

Dynamic difficulty calibration preserving dignity while mapping longitudinal cognitive trends.

Dynamic Difficulty Adjustment Algorithm

Runs locally in platform.js:getAdaptiveDifficulty() after each session without sending telemetry to cloud.

Level Progression (Level +1):
Recent 5-game accuracy ≥ 85% with 0 hints used → Smooth transition to higher stimuli count.
Gentle Support (Level -1):
Recent accuracy < 50% OR hints ≥ 2 → Automatically simplify choice pool to avoid frustration.
Severe Stage Safeguard:
Locks to binary 2-choice tactile buttons, infinite time, and soothing voice guidance.

AI Avatar Subsystem
SLIDE 06 / 10

AI Avatar Companion: Current Prototype vs. Future Vision

Balancing immediate offline rural feasibility today with bleeding-edge photorealistic neural family avatars tomorrow.

Working in Current Build ● ZERO-GPU CLIENT
Current Interactive 3D VRM Pipeline
  • 3-Angle Camera Capture: AvatarOnboardingModal.jsx captures front/left/right photos of family member.
  • Local Canvas Normalization: 512x512 normalized texture generated in browser canvas & stored in IndexedDB.
  • 3D Mesh Rigging: Loaded via Three.js and @pixiv/three-vrm in AvatarCanvas.jsx.
  • Word-Boundary Lip-Sync: Speech synthesis events drive real-time mouth morphs (aa, ih, ou, ee, oh) via visemeMapper.js.
Advantage: Instant 60 FPS on low-cost ₹6,000 tablet, 100% private, zero network requirement.
Future Vision Roadmap ○ PHASE 2 RESEARCH
Photorealistic Neural Family Avatars
  • 3D Gaussian Splatting / FLAME: Reconstruct precise 3D head geometry & facial skin geometry from 5-second video scan.
  • Few-Shot Voice Cloning: Lightweight XTTS-v2 / Chatterbox integration to clone grandchild or son's comforting voice.
  • Neural Real-Time Lip-Sync: LivePortrait / SadTalker diffusion streaming synchronized micro-expressions.
  • Edge Quantization: NPU-accelerated ONNX runtime targeting upcoming rural mobile chipsets.
Goal: True photorealistic visual presence of migrating family members for dementia patients.

Field Workflow & Operations
SLIDE 07 / 10

ASHA Worker Workflow: Multi-Patient Field Operations

Designed for community health workers conducting home visits across Majuli, Bishnupur, and rural districts.

Step 1: Patient Switch
Instant Profile Switch
  • ASHA enters secure PIN ('1234') in CaregiverPinModal.jsx.
  • Selects patient (e.g., Biren Das, 72, Majuli or Memi Devi, 68, Loktak).
  • Platform state dynamically switches active baseline and memory anchors.
Step 2: Guided Session
Assisted Engagement
  • ASHA worker conducts 15-minute gentle daily check-in + 3 game rounds.
  • "Together Mode" enabled: ASHA guides without penalizing response time.
  • Stars collected and fed directly into the patient's local memory garden.
Step 3: Sync & Report
Outbox Batch & Export
  • All metrics logged into local FIFO outbox (syncQueue.js).
  • On reaching PHC with Wi-Fi/4G, outbox auto-flushes in a single batch.
  • Generates printable clinical report PDF for visiting PHC medical officer.

Feasibility & Risk Mitigation
SLIDE 08 / 10

Feasibility, Ethics & Clinical Boundaries

Adhering strictly to Indian healthcare privacy, non-clinical ethics, and low-cost hardware feasibility.

Hardware Feasibility
Low-Cost Edge Devices
  • Runs smoothly on standard quad-core Android tablets (2GB RAM).
  • PWA install size < 8MB with compressed audio assets.
  • Optional AI backend runs on basic dual-core CPU with Faster Whisper INT8 quantization.
Data Privacy & Security
Zero Cloud Leakage
  • Patient photos, voice samples, and daily mood entries NEVER leave the device.
  • Caregiver access strictly gated by PIN lock.
  • One-click complete data wipe (clearPlatformData()) honoring patient sovereignty.
Ethical Boundary
Non-Diagnostic Guarantee
  • Explicitly marked as an engagement tool, NOT a diagnostic medical device.
  • Never uses scary labels like "Dementia Stage 3" in front of older adults.
  • Reports clearly state: "Supports care conversations; does not replace neurologist consultation."
